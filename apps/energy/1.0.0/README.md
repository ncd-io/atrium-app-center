# Energy Monitor

Monitor energy consumption and identify optimization opportunities.

## What it does

- Visualizes energy usage across monitored equipment.
- Highlights consumption trends so you can spot waste and optimization
  opportunities.

## Requirements

- Atrium platform **2.0.0** or newer.
- The **Productivity Monitor** app (Energy Monitor builds on its runtime data).
- Energy/current sensors reporting to the gateway.

## Permissions

- `data:read` — reads telemetry from the platform database (read-only).
